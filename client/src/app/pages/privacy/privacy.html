{{ appName }} is a personal portfolio project that helps people organize a job search. This page explains, in plain language, what information it collects and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never in readable form.
  • Profile details you choose to add: name, phone, location, and LinkedIn or portfolio links.
  • Your job search data: the applications you track, including companies, positions, dates, statuses, and notes.
  • Files you upload: résumés and documents attached to applications.

How it's used

Your information is used only to run {{ appName }} for you: to show your data, store your files, and send emails you need, such as account confirmation, password resets, security notices, and the interview and follow-up reminders you set up.

Who can see it

Your data is private to your account. It is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and {{ appName }} has no ads or third-party tracking. It is stored and processed using Amazon Web Services (in the United States), with Cloudflare providing domain services.

Cookies and browser storage

{{ appName }} uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in securely, and your browser's local storage to remember small preferences, such as hiding the notice at the top of the page. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.

How it's protected

All connections use HTTPS. Data and files are encrypted at rest, the database is not reachable from the internet, and uploaded files are private and accessible only through short-lived links that are created for you after you sign in.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from your profile page. This immediately removes your account, applications, résumés, and uploaded files. Automatic database backups, kept to protect against data loss, are erased on a rolling basis within 7 days.

Sample job listings

The job listings on {{ appName }} are sample data for demonstration. Applying to one records it in your own tracker; nothing is sent to any employer.

Children

{{ appName }} is not intended for anyone under 16.
